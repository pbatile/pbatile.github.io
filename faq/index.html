Who installs tile near me in West Palm Beach?

PBA Tile & Marble installs bathroom, shower, floor, and kitchen tile in West Palm Beach and across Palm Beach County. The shop is at 2635 Meadow Rd, West Palm Beach, FL 33406. Call or text 561-632-7480.

What tile work does PBA Tile & Marble do?

Bathroom tile, shower walls and niches, porcelain and large-format floors, marble floors, and kitchen backsplashes. We also repair flooring, install laminate, and take on commercial tile. We work with homeowners, general contractors, and builders.

How do I ask for a tile estimate?

Call or text 561-632-7480, or email pavzinfo@gmail.com. PBA Tile & Marble schedules a free in-home consultation, reviews the space, discusses materials, and provides a detailed quote.

Monday through Friday, 9:00 AM to 4:30 PM. Closed Saturday and Sunday.

Where is PBA Tile & Marble located?

2635 Meadow Rd, West Palm Beach, FL 33406, United States.

We serve Palm Beach County, including West Palm Beach, Lake Worth Beach, Boynton Beach, Delray Beach, Boca Raton, Wellington, Palm Beach Gardens, Royal Palm Beach, Jupiter, Jupiter Farms, and Loxahatchee.

Do you install shower niches?

Yes. We install luxury shower walls, bathroom tile, niches, and waterproof installations. Finished showers include niches, and some include a tiled bench.
