Tile Installation in Boynton Beach

We install tile in Boynton Beach, on the coast south of Lake Worth Beach and north of Delray Beach and Boca Raton.

Call 561-632-7480 Text for Estimate

PBA Tile & Marble installs tile for Boynton Beach homes from our shop at 2635 Meadow Rd, West Palm Beach. Call or text 561-632-7480, or email pavzinfo@gmail.com.

We install bathroom tile, shower tile with niches and waterproof installations, porcelain, large-format, and marble floors, and kitchen backsplashes. We also handle commercial tile for offices, retail, and restaurants, plus flooring repair and laminate. Homeowners and general contractors hire us for preparation, precise installation, a clean finish, dependable scheduling, and clear communication.

Our shop is in West Palm Beach, and we come to the house in Boynton Beach.

Ask for a visit Monday through Friday, 9:00 AM to 4:30 PM. The consultation is free and in your home, and the quote comes after we have looked at the space.
